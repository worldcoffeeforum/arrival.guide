Arriving by Car

Coex East Gate → B2 Purple Zone → Entrance 3 → Registration desk on 3F is the simplest route.

Coex parking is divided into 4 color-coded zones (Green, Purple, Blue, Orange). For the Forum, please park in the Purple Zone on Basement 2 (B2).

  • Green Zone
  • Purple Zone
  • Blue Zone
  • Orange Zone
  1. On Yeongdong-daero, drive from the COEX Junction toward the Samseong Junction, then turn right into the Coex East Gate (near Halls A and C).
  2. Go down to the Purple Zone on Basement 2 (B2) by following the light-purple guide line.
  3. Park near Entrance 3 in the Purple Zone (if full, the areas around Entrance 4 or 5 also work).
  4. Walk to Entrance 3, then go up to the 3rd floor and collect your badge at the registration desk in front of Halls C1·C2.
  • The Coex area is expected to be crowded on the day, so please allow extra time before your session begins.
  • Please send us your vehicle number in advance for parking registration.
  • If you have a companion, they can get off on the 1st floor first while you park, which is more convenient.
  • When leaving, search your vehicle number at a pre-payment kiosk to find where you parked.
  • Large vehicles (2,500cc or above) will find the Coex West Gate easier to use.
  • Parking inquiries: On-site Parking Support Office, +82-2-6000-7514
STEP1

Head to the 1st floor of Coex

If you take the subway, head toward Coex via the connecting passage from Exit 5/6 of Samseong Station (Line 2) or from Exit 7 of Bongeunsa Station (Line 9). If you arrive from outside, use the East Gate or South Gate 2.

If you entered through the East Gate, keep going straight. The escalator up to the 3rd and 4th floors is on the left side of the corridor ahead. Elevators are on both sides of the South Gate 2 passage.

  • Line 2 Samseong Station, passage toward Exit 5/6
  • Line 9 Bongeunsa Station, Exit 7
STEP2

Collect your badge at the 3F registration desk

On the 3rd floor, follow the signs toward Halls C1·C2 to reach the registration desk and collect your badge.

→3F Registration · Halls C1·C2

On Thursday, Nov. 12, you can also collect your badge at the registration desk in the Room 401 lobby, Conference Rooms (South), 4F.

STEP3

Go to your program room

With your badge, head to the room where your program takes place.

  • #301 The Sessions

About Seoul

Weather

  • Korea has four distinct seasons, and November in Seoul is generally cool to cold.
  • The average temperature in November is around 9°C (48°F), with cooler temperatures in the mornings and evenings.
  • We recommend bringing warm, layered clothing and a jacket or coat.
  • Please check the latest weather forecast shortly before your trip, as temperatures may vary.

About Seoul

Money

  • Korea’s official currency is the Korean won (KRW / ₩).
  • Common banknote denominations are ₩1,000, ₩5,000, ₩10,000, and ₩50,000.
  • Credit cards are widely accepted at hotels, department stores, restaurants, and most shops.
  • Exchange rates fluctuate, so we recommend checking the current exchange rate before your trip.

Electricity

  • Voltage: 220V / 60Hz
  • Socket type: two round-pin outlets are commonly used in Korea.
  • Depending on your device and country of origin, you may need a travel adapter.
  • Travel adapters can usually be purchased at airports, convenience stores, retail stores, or borrowed from some hotels.

About Seoul

Transportation

Seoul has an extensive public transportation network, including subways, buses, and taxis. Information for international visitors is available in English through the Seoul Metropolitan Government and Visit Seoul websites.

A. TAXI

Taxis are widely available and can be hailed on the street or requested through mobile apps. Using an app is recommended, as you can enter your destination in advance and check your driver and vehicle information before pickup.

k.ride by Kakao MobilitySupports English, Chinese, and Japanese, with multilingual destination search and automatic translation chat with drivers.

Uber TaxiThe Uber app can be used to request licensed local taxis in Seoul.

B. SUBWAY

  • One of the most convenient ways to travel around the city. Stations are numbered and color-coded, with multilingual information at major stations and ticket machines.
  • A Tmoney card can be used for subway and bus fares, and can be purchased and reloaded at subway stations and convenience stores.
  • For route planning and real-time information, we recommend NAVER Map, KakaoMap, or the official Seoul subway information services.

C. BUS

  • Seoul’s public bus network connects most areas of the city. A Tmoney card can also be used on buses.
  • Tap your card when both boarding and getting off, particularly when transferring between buses or the subway.
  • Real-time routes and arrival information are available on NAVER Map or KakaoMap. A navigation app is recommended if you are unfamiliar with Seoul.
